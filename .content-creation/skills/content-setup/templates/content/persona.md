# Persona

The one reader you write for. Not a demographic, a person. Every hook has to make them think "that's me", and every skill tests its drafts against them.

Pick one. You'll reach other people too, but writing for everyone means writing for no one. Base it on a real person or a few real conversations if you can. Delete the guidance comments as you fill each section in.
