# Interview question bank

Used by the interview path in `content-setup`. About 25 minutes in total: nine core rounds and one optional round.

## How to run it

- **Rounds of two or three questions.** Never paste the whole bank. Ask a round, wait, then ask the next.
- **Skip what you already know.** If the user linked files or you pulled their posts, drop every question those already answer, and say which ones you skipped.
- **Follow up once on vague answers.** "I help people grow" gets one nudge: "Grow what, for whom? Give me one real example." Then move on; a gap can be filled later.
- **Use their words.** Write their phrasing into the files, not a polished paraphrase. Their phrasing is their voice.
- **Optional rounds are optional.** Offer them; don't push.
- **Let them stop early.** After round 9 there's enough for the three minimum files; round 6 can wait. Ask for at least one story first (round 10, question 1), because every script needs one. Offer to write the files and come back for the rest.
- **Never fill a gap with a guess.** If an answer is missing, leave the guidance comment in the file and list it under "Still to fill".

Each round says which file and section its answers feed.

## Round 1: You and your goal → `creator-ikigai.md` (Snapshot, What people pay for, Channels and goals)

1. In a sentence or two, what do you do, and who do you do it for?
2. What do you want your content to do for you in the next year? (Clients, a job, an audience for a product, sponsorships, something else.)
3. Where do you post now, or want to? Roughly how often?

## Round 2: Your edge and your proof → `creator-ikigai.md` (Your edge, Proof you can cite)

1. What have you lived, built or seen that most people talking about your topic haven't?
2. Which numbers, credentials or results are you happy to say in public? (Years doing it, people helped, a before and after, a title, a result.)
3. Anything about your background that should never show up in content?

## Round 3: Pillars → `creator-ikigai.md` (What you love talking about, Content pillars)

1. What could you talk about for an hour with no notes?
2. What do people ask you about most, in DMs, at work, at dinner?
3. If you had to group everything you post into three or four buckets, what would they be called?

## Round 4: Point of view → `pov.md` (Core POV) and `creator-ikigai.md` (Your Takes)

1. What do most people in your space believe that you think is wrong?
2. What does that wrong belief cost the people who hold it? (This is the trap.)
3. What did you see or learn that changed your mind, and what's possible once someone sees it your way? (The shift and the payoff.)

After this round, draft the core POV in one sentence and read it back. Ask: "Would you put your name on this exactly as written?"

## Round 5: The one reader → `persona.md`

1. Think of one real person you'd most love to help with your content. Describe them: their job, their situation, what they've already tried. Give them a made-up first name, not their real one.
2. What are they stuck on, and what do they want instead? Use the words they'd use.
3. What would they type into a search bar at the end of a bad day?

If they say "everyone" or "founders and creators and marketers", push once: "If only one person could read your next post, who is it?"

## Round 6: Who it's not for, and what scares them → `persona.md` (What scares them, Who this is NOT for, How they like to learn)

1. What is this person afraid of, under the surface?
2. Who are you deliberately not talking to?
3. How does this person like to learn: quick tips or deep dives, video or text?

## Round 7: Voice → `creator-ikigai.md` (Voice)

1. Paste two or three things you've written that sound exactly like you: a post, an email, a message. (If they have posts, prefer these over any self-description.)
2. Three words for how you want to sound. And three words you never want to sound like.
3. Any words, phrases or habits that make you cringe in other people's content? Your rules on emoji, exclamation marks, hashtags, all caps?

From their samples, draft the Voice section yourself: sentence length, how they open, words they repeat, punctuation habits. Quote their own lines as evidence. Then ask them to correct it.

## Round 8: Guardrails and metrics → `creator-ikigai.md` (Guardrails, Metrics you care about)

1. Topics, people or details that are off-limits? (Family, money, clients, health, employers.)
2. Anything you'd never claim, promise or sell?
3. When a post works, which number tells you? Rank your top three. (Saves, shares, follows, comments, leads, subscribers, sales.)

## Round 9: CTAs and logistics → `creator-ikigai.md` (CTAs, handles and logistics)

1. What are your handles, and what's the one sentence you'd end a video on to ask for a follow?
2. Do you have something to send people (a guide, a checklist, a template) and one word they'd comment to get it? "Not yet" is a fine answer.
3. What timezone do you post in, on which days? Which accounts (tools, partners, people) are you happy to tag?

## Round 10: Stories → `story-bank/mine/`

Question 1 is core: scripts and posts need a real moment, and skills can't invent one. Questions 2 and 3 are optional.

1. Tell me one moment that changed how you think about your topic. Where were you, and what was said?
2. A time you got it wrong, and what you do differently now?
3. Is there a story from someone else (a book, an interview, a mentor) you keep retelling? Who should get credit?

Write one story file per story from `story-bank/_template.md`, plus an index row. Keep the specifics; don't tidy them into a lesson.

## Round 11 (optional): Sub-POVs → `pov.md` (Sub-POVs)

1. What are two or three smaller things you believe that most people in your space get wrong?
2. For each: what do people usually think instead, and what's your proof?

## Files to write, and the minimum each needs

| File | Minimum to write it | Rounds |
|---|---|---|
| `creator-ikigai.md` | Snapshot, at least two pillars, a Voice section, Guardrails | 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9 |
| `pov.md` | A one-sentence core POV with the three beats | 4 (11 optional) |
| `persona.md` | Snapshot, what they want, what they're stuck on, three questions they ask | 5, 6 |
| `story-bank/` | At least one story | 10 |
| `README.md` | Always; written last | none |

If a file can't meet its minimum, write what you have, keep the guidance comments in the empty sections, and list it under "Still to fill" at the end.
