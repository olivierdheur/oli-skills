# Starter examples: newsletter formatting

Use these only until the user has winners of their own in `content/winners/newsletter/`. They show how a title pair is built and how a formatted issue is laid out. Copy the moves, never the words, the creator's facts or their proof. The user's details come from their context files.

Two kinds of example below:
- **One real title pair** by Mika Reyes (@its.mikareyes), credited, with the moves labeled.
- **Illustrations** written to show the method cleanly. They are not real issues and their numbers are made up.

---

## Real title pair

Credit: Mika Reyes (@its.mikareyes), Substack

> **How a 1-Hour AI Audit Got Me 653K Search Impressions (Without Being an Expert)**
> A founder's beginner guide to SEO and AEO: the cheat sheet, my 1-hour audit, and 26 foundational fixes.

**Moves:**
- Title carries a number (653K), a time cost (1 hour) and the method (an AI audit), so the claim and the proof arrive together.
- The parenthetical answers the reader's objection ("I'm not an expert") before they raise it.
- Subtitle is on a different axis: who it's for (founders, beginners) and the actual artifacts inside (a cheat sheet, the audit, a list of fixes with a count).
- No question mark, no empty verbs, nothing the article doesn't deliver.

**Leave:** the topic, the numbers and the wording.

## Illustration: three options that differ by angle

For a fictional issue about a physical therapist's 10-minute desk routine:

```
1. **The 10-Minute Routine I Give Every Desk Worker With Back Pain**
   Four moves, one timer and the chair adjustment most people skip.
   → title: number + proof; subtitle: method + artifacts

2. **Stop Stretching Your Hamstrings for Back Pain**
   What 12 years in a clinic taught me to do instead, in ten minutes a day.
   → title: contrarian claim; subtitle: receipt + constraint

3. **How My Patients Fixed Desk-Job Back Pain Without a Gym**
   The routine, the warning signs and when to see someone in person.
   → title: outcome + objection; subtitle: method + failure mode
```

Three angles, not three rewordings. Every number would have to appear in the draft.

```
***

[[SHARE: MIDDLE]]

## <existing subhead>

[[SUBSCRIBE: UPGRADE]]

## <existing subhead>
...
<closing, unchanged>

[[SHARE: END]]

***

```

The publishing notes, delivered separately, hold the suggested button lines, the list of links placed and anything that needs checking.
