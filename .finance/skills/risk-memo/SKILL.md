# Risk Memo

Use this skill to make financial risk explicit and decision-ready.

## Workflow

1. Identify exposure, owner, time horizon, loss mechanism, and control environment.
2. Build scenarios: base, stress, severe, operational failure, liquidity shock,
   regulatory change, and counterparty failure.
3. Map controls, monitoring, escalation, and residual risk.

## Output

Return `Risk summary`, `Scenario table`, `Controls`, `Monitoring`, and
`Escalation plan`.
