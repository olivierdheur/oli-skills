# Fintech Product

Use this skill for fintech product strategy and early design.

## Workflow

1. Define user, transaction flow, funds flow, data flow, and value proposition.
2. Identify regulated activities, partner dependencies, fraud vectors, and
   operational controls.
3. Analyze pricing, unit economics, support burden, and launch sequence.
4. Produce questions for counsel, compliance, banking partners, or risk.

## Output

Return `Product thesis`, `Flow map`, `Risk/compliance questions`, `Unit
economics`, and `Launch sequence`.
