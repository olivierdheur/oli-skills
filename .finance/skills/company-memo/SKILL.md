# Company Memo

Use this skill for structured company research.

## Workflow

1. Identify company, segment, decision, and source hierarchy.
2. Analyze business model, revenue, margins, cash flow, balance sheet, moat,
   competition, management, and catalysts.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, and thesis.
4. Include counterarguments and monitoring indicators.

## Output

Return `Thesis`, `Business overview`, `Drivers`, `Risks`, `Counterarguments`,
and `Monitoring checklist`.
