# Market Brief

Use this skill for market research and update memos.

## Workflow

1. Define market, timeframe, audience, and decision.
2. Gather source hierarchy and separate current data from commentary.
3. Summarize drivers, positioning, catalysts, risks, and uncertainty.
4. Create monitoring indicators and follow-up questions.

## Output

Return `Market summary`, `Key drivers`, `Catalysts`, `Risks`, and `Watchlist`.
